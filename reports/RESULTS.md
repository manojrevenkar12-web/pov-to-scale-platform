## Reading the ablations

- **Without budgets** the platform keeps isolation and the promotion gate but lets runaway agents and leaked keys spend: €285k unapproved a year.
- **Without the promotion gate** 6.2 regressed releases a year reach production instead of 0.1; the gate costs 14.3 stopped promotions and about a week each.
- **What the platform costs:** 34.7 days a year in which a PoV waited for its owner to raise a budget, and running the platform itself (included in engineer time).
